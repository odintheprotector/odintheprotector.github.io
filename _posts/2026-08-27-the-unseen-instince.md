The story starts with an assassin. He grew up under the crushing weight of judgmental eyes. Even as a child with nothing in his hands, he was used to people looking down on him. They laughed at him, showing off their expensive, shiny weapons, thinking a small kid like him was useless.

But those mean looks actually made him stronger. He did not have high-tech weapons; he only had a simple sword. To survive among people who wanted to crush him, he had to learn how to read their minds. He learnt to sense danger hiding behind arrogant smiles. 
He understood that to beat enemies who think they are better than you, you must get close. You have to see their pride, their fear and how they breathe. 

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b95ee6df-7f94-48ef-af90-d2202b7ce28d)

So he grew up to become a famous zero-range fighter. People said that once he hid in the dark and pulled out his knife, no one could survive to return and tell to others about their battles. 

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7aa75a38-4fe4-4038-a2c9-cfe4091d944b)

Before this, he worked for a few big organizations. The hiring process was always the same. There was always a dark room smelling of smoke, a boss pushing a lot of money across the table, smiling like a king:

_"Take the job, Just follow our rules. We will take care of everything, your life, your future. You will never have to worry"_

They money and the sweet promises sounded nice at first. But the longer he stayed, the more he felt like he was in a cage. He was just a tool taking orders. The rules were too strict and bring. The system was slowly making his sharp sword dull. Knowing he did not belong there anymore, he decided to leave. The day he quit, the boss stopped smiling. He looked at him with cold eyes and said: _"If you are smart, you will stay. If you walk out that door... you will pay with your life. Do you think a knife-fighter like you can survive out there on your own?"_

It was not a joke. That same night, they started hunting him. They sent their worst killers to silence him. Footsteps in the dark, blades flying in the night... it felt exactly like how real life attacks someone who just quit their job. It was the stress of needing money, the bad rumors people about you and the feeling that society is judging you. But the forgot he was the king of zero-range combat. By morning, the fight was over. He wiped the blood off his face, stepped over the enemies and took a deep breath of freedom. 

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c3d76fd3-b0b1-444d-8646-9e2af663b8fb)

However, finding a new home was just as hard. The outside world had changed. When he took his resume, written with his battle scars to new places, they all said no. In this modern time, everyone loved guns. They loved fast, automatic rifles that could shoot targets from far away in a second. They looked at his knife and his zero-range style, and laughed at him just like when he was a kid: _"Who still uses a knife? Now, you just press a button. Your style is too old."_

Those words hurt his pride. During the lonely nights, he started to doubt himself: _"Are they right? Is there really no place for me in a world full of automatic machines?"_

But when his mind was clear, he realized a big truth that those gun lovers could not see. Guns can shoot fast from far away, but guns do not have human intuition. An automatic gun cannot see a trap in a dark room. A cold machine cannot smell the danger behind a fake smile. And when the enemy plays mind games to trick you, automatic guns are completely useless. That thought gave him strength. Finally, a real organization, one that truly understood how to survive, opened its doors to him. When he walked in, they treated him with respect. 
They did not ask him to drop his knife to hold a gun. They did not put him behind a desk. He was still a fighter; he was still the one standing on the front lines. But they paid him for something else: his intelligence and his deep understanding of people. They knew that when the automatic machines fail, the team needs his human eyes and intuition. He jumps into the most dangerous places, using his real experience to break the traps in a blink of an eye. He fights next to the younger guys. He uses his sharp skills and scars to cover the 
mistakes of the machines. He teaches the young generation a very important lesson: **technology is fast, but to survive the tricks of this world, you still need the smart brain and human feelings of a real person.**

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4d730be8-c6cb-4353-be5f-4b2a484e469c)

I believe that you used to be that person. You found your life struggle, works were not suitable anymore, and you decided to step into the unknown. You walked away from a place where you no longer belonged. Sure, people might have judged you, and you might have faced many lonely, scary nights trying to find your new path. But you did not give up. You kept moving forward until you found a place that truly values your real experience, a place where you can use your sharp mind to guide others. So, if you ever doubt your choices, remember this story. 
The scariest thing is not making a change. The scariest thing is hiding in a dark room, living a boring life, and letting your skills rust away forever. Leaving is not scary; standing still is the real death sentence. Trusting your process! I wish you would be fine in your decision! Keep walking!
